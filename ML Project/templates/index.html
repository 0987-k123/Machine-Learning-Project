🏆 Diagnostic Winner: Random Forest Classifier

Random Forest achieved superior diagnostic accuracy, balanced F1-Score, and discriminative power (ROC-AUC) compared to Logistic Regression. In medical diagnostic screening, Random Forest excels at isolating non-linear threshold effects in HbA1c and Blood Glucose levels with minimal false negatives.

Why Sensitivity (Recall) is the Critical Clinical KPI

In asymptomatic diabetes screening, the clinical cost of a False Negative (failing to diagnose an active diabetic patient) is severe. Delayed diagnosis leads to irreversible microvascular damage: retinopathy (blindness), nephropathy (kidney failure), and neuropathy.

Conversely, a False Positive only requires an inexpensive, non-invasive confirmatory fasting plasma glucose or oral glucose tolerance test.

Conclusion: Random Forest minimizes false negatives while maintaining high precision.

Architectural Trade-Off Comparison

  • Logistic Regression: Linear decision boundary in log-odds space. Highly interpretable through odds ratios and fast inference (<1.5ms), but assumes monotonic linear relationships without threshold jumps.
  • Random Forest: Non-parametric ensemble of decorrelated decision trees that naturally segments non-linear interactions (e.g. sharp diabetic tipping points when HbA1c exceeds 6.5% and Glucose > 140 mg/dL).
  • SMOTE Balancing: Synthetic oversampling prevented majority-class bias without discarding valuable real-world negative cases.
